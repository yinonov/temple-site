# The Temple Mount, now: a living Herodian Temple Mount (public build)

Built site only. A place you visit rather than a game you play: the Temple Mount runs on the real Jerusalem clock
(seasonal hours, Shabbat and festivals) and everyone who opens it at the same moment sees the same moment.

- What the experience states is tied to the public-domain Hebrew Mishnah (Vilna 1913, as digitised by Sefaria); place notes
  say where each fact comes from. Faces, colours, crowds, routes and the hours in between are imagined, and marked so.
- Sound is synthesised in the browser, plus spoken lines generated at authoring time. The stone and paving textures are CC0 (ambientCG) and
  the props and wall pieces are our own Blender work; no fonts are shipped (see `content/credits.md`).
- Code: MIT (`LICENSE`); three.js: MIT (`vendor/three/LICENSE`).
- A playground: it may change or break without notice.
