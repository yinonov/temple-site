## Not covered by this licence

Quoted third-party texts keep their own licences and attributions, listed in `NOTICE.md` and in the
site's "מקורות ורישיונות" dialog; the verification record is kept in the project's source repository. In particular:

- Rabbi Dan Be'eri's Kaufmann-based Mishnah excerpts stay under **CC BY-SA 3.0**, individually labelled.
- Dr. Joshua Kulp's English Mishnah (Mishnah Yomit) is labelled CC BY by Sefaria; the rights holder's confirmation is
  outstanding. It is therefore **not included in the public website build**.

Ancient texts themselves (Mishnah, Talmud, Tanakh, Josephus) are not owned by anyone; the licences above concern the
specific editions and digital transcriptions.
